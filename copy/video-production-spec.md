## Executive Summary

A premium, cinematic 14-16 second background video designed for scroll-driven website implementation. The video features three seamless visual phases showcasing professional window cleaning equipment, pristine material details, and the aspirational result of expert exterior cleaning.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Premium, minimal, cinematic aesthetic
- Very slow, smooth camera movements (suitable for frame-by-frame scrubbing)
- Professional lighting with warm amber rim light
- Navy/teal color palette with warm golden-hour grading
- No people, logos, text, or visual clutter
- Negative space on sides for website text overlays
- 16:9 aspect ratio, 1080p minimum (4K recommended)

## Camera Movement Guidelines

### Principles
1. **Slow & Meditative:** All camera movements should take 4–6 seconds minimum
2. **Smooth:** No jittery, jerky, or sudden changes in direction
3. **Intentional:** Every movement serves a visual purpose
4. **Subtle:** Movements should feel natural, not dramatic or showy
5. **Scroll-Friendly:** Compatible with frame-by-frame scrubbing (no motion blur artifacts)

### Specific Techniques

#### Phase 1: Push-In
- **Start:** Wide establishing shot (equipment + windows visible)
- **End:** Macro details (polished metal, pristine glass, water droplets)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Easing:** Linear or very gentle ease-in at end
- **Technique:** Smooth optical zoom or camera dolly forward

#### Phase 2: Macro Rotation
- **Technique:** Gentle orbital camera rotation around droplets
- **Direction:** Counterclockwise (subtle)
- **Axis:** Vertical (rotation around center point)
- **Amplitude:** Small (±15–20° total rotation)
- **Duration:** 4.5 seconds
- **Speed Variation:** Constant or very subtle ease

#### Phase 3: Lateral Pan
- **Direction:** Left-to-right (or right-to-left for balance)
- **Duration:** 3.5–5.5 seconds
- **Speed:** Constant, very slow
- **Amplitude:** Full width of facade (approximately 60–80° camera angle shift)
- **Easing:** Linear or very gentle ease

### Prohibited Movements
- ❌ Fast zooms or pans
- ❌ Jerky or sudden direction changes
- ❌ Shaky/handheld camera effect
- ❌ Rapid focus changes
- ❌ Spinning or rotating on multiple axes simultaneously
- ❌ Motion blur or speed ramping effects

## Lighting Specifications

### Color Temperature & Tone
- **Primary Light:** Warm golden-hour sunlight (color temp: 3500–4500K)
- **Tone:** Warm amber/orange cast throughout
- **Secondary Light:** Soft cool skylight (fill, reduces contrast)
- **Overall Feel:** Warm, inviting, premium, professional

### Light Angles
- **Phase 1 (Equipment):** 45° side-right angle, creating rim light on metals
- **Phase 2 (Droplets):** 45° side angle with top-down component, maximizing refraction
- **Phase 3 (Workspace):** Frontal/overhead (sunlight through windows), creating bright, clear illumination

### Intensity & Contrast
- **Contrast Ratio:** High but not harsh (3:1 to 4:1)
- **Shadow Quality:** Soft, well-defined shadows (not pure black)
- **Highlight Detail:** Preserved in bright areas (no blown-out whites)
- **Fill Light:** Sufficient to see detail in shadows (dark areas still visible)

### Special Lighting Techniques
- **Rim Light (Amber):** Used on equipment and droplets to emphasize premium materials and clarity
- **Bokeh:** Soft-focus background in Phases 1 & 2, created by shallow depth of field and rim light
- **Light Refraction:** In Phase 2, light passing through water droplets creating rainbow spectrum
- **Reflection:** In Phase 3, landscape and sky reflected in windows creating depth and clarity

## Color Grading Specification

### Color Palette (Brand Colors)
- **Primary:** Navy Dark (#0D1B2A) — Used in shadows, dark areas
- **Accent:** Teal (#0EA5B5) — Subtle accents in highlights
- **Secondary Accent:** Cyan Light (#67D1E0) — Very subtle in bright areas
- **Neutral:** Gray (#6B7280) — Used sparingly in mid-tones

### Grading Direction
- **Saturation:** Warm colors boosted (oranges, ambers); cool colors slightly desaturated
- **Desaturation:** Slight overall desaturation (10–15%) for premium, refined look
- **Shadows:** Slight cyan/teal cast in shadows (complementary to warm highlights)
- **Highlights:** Warm amber tone preserved; no cool color shifts
- **Midtones:** Balanced, slightly warm

### Specific Color Adjustments
1. **Oranges/Warm Tones:** +15% saturation (sunlight, equipment, rim light)
2. **Blues/Sky:** +10% saturation and contrast (makes sky pop)
3. **Cyans/Teals:** +5–10% opacity in shadows (subtle brand color)
4. **Overall:** -10% to -15% saturation for refined, premium aesthetic
5. **Contrast:** Lifted blacks slightly (+10–15% in shadows) for detail preservation

### Look & Feel
- **Overall Aesthetic:** Luxury residential/commercial real estate videography
- **Mood:** Premium, professional, trustworthy, aspirational
- **Temperature:** Warm (golden-hour inspired throughout)
- **Clarity:** Crystal clear, high-definition, detail-rich
- **Atmosphere:** SoCal coastal luxury (bright, clean, modern)

## Composition & Framing

### Aspect Ratio
- **Format:** 16:9 (landscape, standard web video)
- **Safe Area:** Leave negative space on left and right (20% on each side) for website text overlays

### Safe Text Area
- **Left Side:** Clear space for headlines and CTA (0–20% of width)
- **Right Side:** Clear space for secondary text/badges (80–100% of width)
- **Center:** Strong visual content without text interference (30–70% of width)

### Composition Principles
- **Rule of Thirds:** Use for main visual elements (equipment in Phase 1, droplets in Phase 2, workspace in Phase 3)
- **Leading Lines:** Use window frames, equipment edges to guide viewer eye
- **Negative Space:** Generous empty space for overlaid text
- **Depth:** Use shallow depth of field (Phases 1–2) and layering (Phase 3) to create dimensional feel
- **Balance:** Asymmetrical balance (not centered, not too off-center)

### Foreground / Background / Depth
- **Phase 1:** Sharp foreground (equipment), soft background (windows/landscape)
- **Phase 2:** Ultra-sharp micro details (droplets), very soft background (tools, surfaces)
- **Phase 3:** Sharp mid-ground (windows, interior), clear background (landscape, sky)
