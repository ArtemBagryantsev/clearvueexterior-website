## 9. Product & Service Description

### **Window Cleaning**
Professional exterior and interior window cleaning using professional-grade equipment and purified water-fed pole systems. We remove dirt, dust, fingerprints, and environmental buildup to restore crystal-clear, streak-free glass.

**Equipment & Methods:**
- **Tacker water-fed poles** – Industry-leading poles for safe, efficient access to high windows
- **Tacker premium brushes** – Soft-bristle brushes designed for delicate surfaces and optimal cleaning
- **Sorbo squeegees** – Professional-grade squeegees for streak-free, crystal-clear finishes
- **Moerman t-bar** – Premium water management tool for precise control and spotless results
- Purified water systems for flawless finishes (no mineral residue)
- Eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions
- Professional-grade microfiber cloths for final polish
- Traditional hand-washing for delicate or specialty windows

**Ideal For:** Homeowners, storefronts, office buildings, multi-story properties

### **Solar Panel Cleaning**
Solar panels naturally collect dust, pollen, bird droppings, and environmental debris. Even small buildup reduces efficiency and energy output. We safely clean panels using purified water and specialized soft-bristle brushes designed specifically for solar panel maintenance.

**Materials & Approach:**
- Purified water (no hard water minerals)
- Soft-bristle brushes to protect panel surface
- Deionized water rinse for spotless results
- Safe, low-pressure methods
- Regular maintenance extends panel lifespan

**Ideal For:** Homeowners and businesses with rooftop solar systems looking to maximize energy efficiency

### **Screen Cleaning**
Window screens naturally accumulate dust, pollen, insects, and debris. We carefully remove screens, thoroughly wash both sides, and reinstall them. Clean screens improve airflow, reduce dust inside the home, and complement freshly cleaned windows.

**Materials & Approach:**
- Careful removal and reinstallation
- Thorough washing with soft brushes
- Inspection for damage or repairs needed
- Professional reinstallation

**Ideal For:** Homeowners and renters who want complete window cleanliness

### **Track Cleaning**
Window tracks are hidden dirt collectors—accumulating dust, insects, pollen, leaves, and debris. This buildup reduces window operation and detracts from appearance. We thoroughly vacuum and clean tracks to improve both functionality and aesthetics.

**Materials & Approach:**
- Powerful vacuum extraction
- Soft brush detailing
- Degreaser for stubborn buildup
- Improved window operation upon completion

**Ideal For:** All window types; especially important for homes with sliding windows and doors

### **Soft Washing (Coming Soon)**
Soft washing is a low-pressure cleaning method for delicate exterior surfaces such as stucco, painted siding, fences, patios, and outdoor living areas. This service will be added as ClearVue Exterior continues to grow.

## 15. Higgsfield Video Brief – Cinematic Hero Video

### **Video Concept: "The Clarity You Deserve"**

A premium, cinematic 15-30 second background video that establishes the ClearVue Exterior brand through visual storytelling. This video plays as the hero background on the homepage and communicates professionalism, quality, and the transformative power of expert exterior cleaning.

---

### **Scene Overview**

**Setting:** A modern luxury home office or creative workspace in Orange County, Southern California. Bright, open, minimalist aesthetic with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a manicured lawn and blue sky.

**Time of Day:** Golden hour (late afternoon) or bright midday with strong, directional sunlight that creates dramatic reflections on glass surfaces.

---

### **Visual Elements & Shots**

**Shot 1: Macro Detail of Water Droplets (0–3 seconds)**
- Extreme close-up of water droplets on a perfectly clean window glass
- Sunlight refracts through each droplet, creating subtle rainbow prisms
- Soft focus background shows blurred outdoor landscape
- Smooth camera movement: slow push-in or gentle rotation around the droplets
- **Mood:** Captures the essence of clarity and precision

**Shot 2: Window Reflection Transition (3–6 seconds)**
- Pull back from the macro shot to reveal the reflection of a modern home/office in the glass
- The reflection is crystal clear, showing the sharp detail of the architecture and outdoor scenery
- Soft sunlight creates subtle glints and sparkles across the glass surface
- **Mood:** Demonstrates the transformative power of clean windows

**Shot 3: Professional Equipment Close-Up (6–10 seconds)**
- Macro shot of professional cleaning tools: soft-bristle brush, purified water droplets, stainless steel equipment
- Hand-held shot showing a gloved hand expertly handling a water-fed pole or professional squeegee
- Water cascades gently across glass, leaving a pristine, streak-free surface
- Bubbles and water droplets catch the light
- **Mood:** Emphasizes craftsmanship and professional-grade quality

**Shot 4: Solar Panel Glint & Shine (10–14 seconds)**
- Wide shot of residential solar panels mounted on a modern home rooftop
- Panels gleam in bright sunlight, reflecting the sky
- Macro shot zooms in on individual solar cells, showing their crystalline surface clean and perfect
- Subtle particle effects: faint sound-wave or energy visualizations ripple across the panel surface (very subtle, premium feel)
- **Mood:** Connects to energy efficiency, cleanliness, and modern living

**Shot 5: Wide Establishing Shot – The Perfect View (14–20 seconds)**
- Pull back to a wide shot of the entire modern home with floor-to-ceiling windows
- Windows are perfectly clean, reflecting the bright Southern California sky and manicured landscape
- The home looks premium, well-maintained, aspirational
- Soft, warm light bathes the scene
- Camera moves slowly (slow push-in or lateral pan) across the façade
- **Mood:** Shows the end result—a premium home that looks impeccable

**Shot 6: Subtle Brand Moment (20–25 seconds)**
- Optional: Brief, very subtle visual of the ClearVue Exterior logo or icon (checkmark + star) appearing for 1–2 seconds as a light overlay or reflection in the glass
- Alternatively: Keep the focus purely on the visual of the clean home and let the website deliver the brand messaging
- **Mood:** Brand association without feeling like an ad

**Shot 7: Fade to Black / Brand Call (25–30 seconds)**
- Gentle fade to black or fade to the website's background
- Tagline appears in elegant typography: "Cleaner Views. Brighter Living."
- Or: Cut directly to the website hero section
